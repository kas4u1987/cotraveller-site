# Support

CoTraveller is a trip planner for iPhone that plans your trip and stays with you through it.

## Get help

[Open an issue](https://github.com/kas4u1987/cotraveller-site/issues). Say which iPhone and iOS version you use, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

## Common questions

**Planning says it needs Apple Intelligence.**
Planning on the device needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Without it the app still works; you plan the days yourself.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Your trips are kept on your phone.

**What is online planning?**
An optional way to plan with larger models through your own OpenRouter account. It is off unless you turn it on. See the [privacy policy](privacy) for what it sends.

**Why does the app ask for location "Always"?**
Only if you turn on "Follow my trip in the background" for a trip. It lets the app notice when you reach and leave a stop with the app closed. Your position never leaves your phone.

**How do I move my trips to a new phone?**
Settings, Backup: share the backup file, then restore it on the new phone.
