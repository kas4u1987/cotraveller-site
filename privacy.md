# Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

CoTraveller is a trip planner for iPhone. It is made by an independent developer ("I"). This page says what the app does with your information.

## The short version

- Your trips, travellers, bookings and notes are kept on your phone.
- There is no account to create, and I run no server. I never receive your trips or anything about you.
- The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- A few features ask an outside service for information. Each one is listed below with exactly what it is sent.

## What stays on your phone

- **Trips, days, stops, notes, travellers, groups and bookings.** Stored in the app on your phone only. iCloud sync is not turned on.
- **Planning with Apple Intelligence.** When you plan a trip on the device, the planning runs on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Your location.** Used to show where you are on the trip map, to tell you when a stop is close or you may be late, and, only on trips where you turn on "Follow my trip in the background", to note when you reach and leave a stop. Your position never leaves your phone. You can turn location off in Settings.
- **Reminders.** Scheduled on your phone by the app. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Bookings you paste or share in.** Read on your phone.
- **Backups.** A backup is one file you create and share yourself. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

## What is sent, and to whom

| Feature | Sent to | What is sent | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Place search, routes, drive times, places along a drive, fuel stations | Apple Maps | The places, areas and roads being searched | When you search or plan |
| Cover photo for a trip | Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons | The destination's name | When a trip is created |
| Theme park attractions | ThemeParks.wiki | The park you pick | When you pick a park |
| Online planning (off unless you turn it on) | OpenRouter, and the model provider it picks | The destination, dates, interests, pace and budget of the trip, how many people are going and their food and other preferences, the places on the itinerary, and any note you write for a day | Only while online planning is on |
| Trip research (part of online planning) | OpenRouter's web search | The destination and dates | Only when you tap to look it up |
| "Open Google Flights" and "Open Google Hotels" | Google, in your browser | The route and dates in the link | Only when you tap the link |

Names, contacts, your location and photos are never sent for online planning. Online planning uses your own OpenRouter account, which you sign in to and pay for. OpenRouter's own privacy policy applies to what it receives. Providers that keep prompts for training are not used.

Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple Maps and Apple Intelligence. The services above receive your device's network address, as any website does.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Your choices

- Delete a trip, traveller or booking in the app, and it is gone from your phone.
- Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.
- Online planning, location and reminders can each be turned off.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
